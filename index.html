What families ask us

Something missing? Write to us and we'll answer it here too.

Does it cost anything?
No. Every event is free to every family. Nobody who teaches is paid.
Do we need to sign up for a library event?
No. Library events are open to anyone. Turn up, or write first if you'd like to know what to expect.
My child isn't a "math kid." Is this for them?
Yes. If your child is anywhere between bored in math class and obsessed with it, this is for them.
What ages is it for?
Library events in Albany are for elementary kids. Neighborhood events are for middle and high schoolers, so everyone works on problems that fit.
Who is teaching?
More than a dozen volunteer instructors, all students at high schools in Albany and Athens. A mathematics professor at Ohio University and several PhD students advise them.
What is the Guild, and how do we apply?
The Guild is a group of ten students who meet online every week and in person once a month, and who compete in Math Kangaroo in March. Applications open once a year. Leave your email below and we'll tell you when.

Why this exists

Most kids don’t dislike math. They dislike the version they were given.

A local school eliminated its gifted program. After that, a kid around here who loved math had nowhere to take it: no team, no club, no competition. The talent stayed. The path went away.

So a few of us built one, starting with puzzles at the library.

Two students who come to the Puzzle Circle have since been named National Math Stars. That's the point. The ability was already here, in ordinary classrooms in a rural county, waiting for someone to hand it a harder problem.
