Terms of use

The rules for using this site and our puzzles, in plain words.

Last updated November 7, 2026

The short version: our events and this website are free. Use the puzzles at home or in class. Kids under 13 need a parent or guardian to contact us for them.

Who we are

The Appalachian Puzzle Circle is a volunteer-run math program for kids in Athens County, Ohio. "We" and "us" on this page mean the program and its volunteers.

Using this site

You're welcome to browse the site, try the puzzles and contact us. Please don't try to break the site, send spam through the form, or pretend to be someone else when you write to us.

Kids under 13 shouldn't use the sign-up form. A parent or guardian should fill it out for them. See our children's privacy policy.

The puzzles

You can use the puzzles on this site at home or in a classroom, and print them for your students. Please ask us before republishing them somewhere else.

Events and Math Captains

Our events and Math Captains are free. Taking part is voluntary, and a parent or guardian decides whether their child takes part. Event dates, places and plans can change, and we'll let you know when they do.

Donations

Gifts go to the costs listed on our Math Captains budget. We're fiscally sponsored by a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, so gifts made through our sponsor are tax-deductible. Gifts made straight to us, outside the sponsor, are not. See the donate page for details.

No guarantees

We work hard to keep this site accurate, but it's provided as it is. We aren't responsible for problems caused by using it, or for other websites we link to.

Changes

We may update these terms. The date at the top shows the latest version.
