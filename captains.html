Math Captains

Ahoy! Math Captains is a crew of ten students from the Alexander district who want more math than school gives them. They train all year, sail into real competitions, and never pay a cent.

Ahoy Alexander district

Ahoy, future captains

Alexander eliminated its gifted program. The open library events give any kid a taste of real problem solving, but a few want to keep going, and around here there was no team, club or competition for them to go to.

Math Captains is that next step. The crew stays small on purpose, so every captain gets real attention from the instructors and a seat at every competition we enter.

On board All of it free

What every captain gets on board

Families pay nothing. Grants and donations cover all of it.

A weekly check-in
An online session every week with an instructor and the rest of the group, to work problems and talk them through.
A monthly meetup
A longer in-person session once a month at the Athens branch of the Athens County Public Library.
Practice booklets
Six Math Kangaroo practice booklets for each student, plus Beast Academy Puzzles books and past contest problems to work through at sessions.
Competition entry
Math Kangaroo registration, paid for, plus a ride to the testing site and a meal that day. We also plan to enter a MOEMS team.
Materials at every session
Puzzle and game sets, mini whiteboards and printed problem packets.
Instructors and advisors
Student volunteer instructors from Albany and Athens high schools, backed by a mathematics professor at Ohio University and several PhD students.
The voyage Year one and two

The voyage

  1. Once a year

    Sign on

    Students apply to join the crew, and ten get a spot for the year. Leave your email and we'll tell you when they open.

  2. Every week

    Train the crew

    Online check-ins every week and an in-person session every month, working through problems a little harder than the last.

  3. March 18, 2027

    First port: Math Kangaroo

    The year's main competition. For most of the group it will be the first math competition they have ever entered. We may also enter a team in MOEMS, the Mathematical Olympiads for Elementary and Middle Schools.

  4. Year two

    Next horizon: AMC 8

    Captains who are ready move up to the AMC 8, the Mathematical Association of America's contest for middle school students.
