Ways to give

  • Online

    Give any amount by card. Donate online

  • Write to us

    Tell us how much you'd like to give and we'll send the details for paying by check or online. Email wanghardy10@gmail.com, or use the form on the front page and tick "Funding the program".

    Math Kangaroo practice booklets, Beast Academy Puzzles books, puzzle and game sets, mini whiteboards and markers. Write first so we can tell you what we still need.

    $152 covers one Math Captain for the year. We'll send you a note on how the year went.

    If your organization funds math or education in Appalachian Ohio, we'll send the full budget and a short report at the end of the year.

Good to know Tax status

Tax status

We're fiscally sponsored by a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, so gifts made through our sponsor are tax-deductible. Write to us before you give and we'll send the sponsor's details. You make the gift out to the sponsor with our name in the memo, and the sponsor sends your tax receipt.

Gifts made straight to us, outside the sponsor, are not tax-deductible. We're also working toward our own 501(c)(3) status.
