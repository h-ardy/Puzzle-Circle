Patterns

What number comes next: 2, 6, 12, 20, and then?

Show a hint
Look at the gaps between the numbers. Then look at the dot rectangles.
Show the answer
30. The gaps are 4, 6 and 8, so the next gap is 10. Or read the rectangles: 1×2, 2×3, 3×4, 4×5, and next comes 5×6.

Logic

A farm has chickens and cows. Together they have 20 heads and 56 legs. How many cows are on the farm?

20heads56legs

Show a hint
Pretend every animal is a chicken. How many legs would that be, and how many are missing?
Show the answer
8 cows. Twenty chickens would have 40 legs, so 16 legs are missing. Each cow adds 2 legs over a chicken, so 16 ÷ 2 = 8 cows, and 12 chickens.

Counting

A pawpaw has six seeds in a row. Each seed is dark or light. How many different rows can there be?

Show a hint
Start small. One seed gives 2 rows. Two seeds give 4. What happens each time you add a seed?
Show the answer
64. Each new seed doubles the count: 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 64. Our badge shows one of them, 101101.
