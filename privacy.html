Privacy policy

What we collect, why, and how to ask us to delete it. This page also covers cookies and children's privacy.

Last updated November 7, 2026

The short version: we only collect what you type into our sign-up form, we use it to write back to you, and we never sell or share it. This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no ads.

What we collect

The only personal information this site collects is what you type into the sign-up form on the front page:

  • who is filling it out (a parent, a student 13 or older, a teacher, or someone else)
  • your name and email address
  • what you'd like to hear about
  • your child's grade, if you choose to tell us
  • anything you write in the note

We don't ask for addresses, phone numbers, birthdays or photos online.

How we use it

We use it to reply to you: to send the next event date, Math Captains application news, or an answer to your question. We don't sell it, rent it or share it with anyone else, and we don't use it for advertising.

If we ever start a mailing list, every email will include a way to unsubscribe and our mailing address, and we'll stop emailing you as soon as you ask.

Services that handle it

When you press Send, the form goes to Formspree, a form service that stores the message and emails it to us. The page doesn't contact Formspree until you press Send.

The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log the IP address of visitors for security. We don't get those logs and don't use them.

Cookies and tracking

This site doesn't set any cookies. It has no analytics, no session recording, no ads and no social media trackers, so there's nothing to accept or turn off.

Our fonts and images are stored on this site, so your browser doesn't contact Google or any other font or image service when you visit.

Children's privacy

Our program is for kids, but this website is meant for their parents, teachers and older students. We don't knowingly collect personal information online from children under 13.

  • The sign-up form asks who is filling it out. If someone says they're a kid under 13, the form stops and asks them to have a parent or guardian fill it out instead.
  • Parents can tell us their child's grade, but please don't put your child's full name or other details in the note. We'll ask for what we need directly from you.
  • If we learn we've received personal information from a child under 13 without a parent's permission, we'll delete it.
  • Parents and guardians can ask to see or delete anything we have about their child at any time.
  • We don't post photos of kids online without written permission from a parent or guardian.

How long we keep it

We keep your message as long as we need it to stay in touch about what you asked for. You can ask us to delete it at any time, and we'll delete it from our email and from Formspree.

Your choices

Email wanghardy10@gmail.com to see what we have about you or your child, correct it, or delete it. We'll reply within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If a change affects how we use information you've already given us, we'll email you first.
